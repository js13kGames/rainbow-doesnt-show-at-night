## Title
Rainbow doesn't show at night?

## Description (full)
A day/night puzzle-platformer built for the Unicorns and Rainbows theme. Flip between day and night to reposition portals, throw switches to raise rainbow bridges, and collect every key before the gate will open. Patrolling obstacles change their route depending on the time of day — time your crossing right, or start over.

## Description (short)
Flip day and night to raise rainbow bridges, dodge obstacles, and find the key to the portal.
