A day/night puzzle-platformer. Flip between day and night to get to your portals, throw switches to raise rainbow bridges, and collect every key before the gate will open. Patrolling obstacles change their route depending on the time of day: time your crossing right, or start over.

[Keys]  
WASD: move  
Space: jump  
J: toggle day and night(tiles differ!)
